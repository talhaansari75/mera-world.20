# Privacy Policy — Mera Word Search Journey

**Last updated: September 28, 2026**

## Introduction

Mera Word Search Journey ("the Game", "we", "us") is developed by Talha Ansari. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you play our game.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Display name** (optional, for leaderboards)
- **Email address** (only if you choose to sign in with Google/Facebook)
- **Guest account ID** (auto-generated, no personal data)

### Information Collected Automatically
- **Game progress** (levels completed, stars earned, coins)
- **Device information** (model, OS version, language)
- **Crash reports** (anonymous, to fix bugs)
- **Analytics** (which levels are played, session length — anonymous)

### We DO NOT Collect
- Your location
- Your contacts
- Your photos or media
- Your phone number
- Any data from other apps

## How We Use Information

- To save your game progress
- To sync progress across devices (if signed in)
- To show relevant ads (if ads are enabled)
- To fix bugs and improve gameplay
- To send notifications (only if you opt in)

## Third-Party Services

The Game may use:

- **Google Play Services** — for leaderboards and achievements
- **Google Firebase** — for cloud save and analytics
- **Ad networks** — if ads are shown (rewarded video ads)

Each service has its own privacy policy:
- Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Children's Privacy

The Game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us information, contact us at the email below.

## Data Security

We use industry-standard security to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

## Your Rights

You can:
- **Delete your account** at any time from Settings
- **Request your data** by emailing us
- **Opt out of analytics** in Settings
- **Disable notifications** in Settings

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Continued use of the Game means you accept the updated policy.
